Overview

Purpose

The purpose for this site is to serve as an interactive archive of my current and past fiber arts projects. Some posts will have only images with a description, while others will also include pattern details.

Audience

The audience of this site are anyone looking for either inspiration for their own yarning projects or for specific patterns.

Dynamic elements

I plan on having each post on the home page link to a seperate page for that post. Those individual pages will have images that will open as modals. I will also include a search function on the home page to help people find specific posts.
